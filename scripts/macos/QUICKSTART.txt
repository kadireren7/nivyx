Nivyx for macOS 11 or later

Install:    double-click "Install Nivyx.command" and enter your password.
            Websites and apps then work normally; nothing else to set up.
Update:     double-click "Update Nivyx.command".
Uninstall:  double-click "Uninstall Nivyx.command" (your settings are kept).

Check it:   open Terminal and run  nivyx status
Problems:   nivyx doctor

If macOS says the file cannot be opened: right-click it, choose Open,
then Open again.
More: https://github.com/kadireren7/nivyx
